Hybrid Fuzzy-CNN Architecture for Low-Light Image Enhancement and Noise Optimization

Published in: IEEE International Conference on Smart Computation & Artificial Intelligence

Executive Abstract

"Digital images captured under severe underexposure conditions suffer from elevated sensor noise, compressed dynamic range, and lost detail. This paper proposes a hybrid architecture combining fuzzy membership functions with a dual-stream Convolutional Neural Network. Experimental results demonstrate significant improvements in PSNR and SSIM over state-of-the-art Retinex models without color artifact distortion."
